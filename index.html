Hi, I'm Benevolence Ed-Malik.

I'm a student getting my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science at Kennesaw State University. I have my Associate Degree in Computer Programming from Chattahoochee Technical College. That ignited my passion for: web and app design and development, data analysis and reporting, and UI/UX design. I believe in the importance of being a creator as well as a consumer of what you love. I am mostly self-taught and I enjoy making websites, apps, visualization reports and I am currently in search of internships where I can learn new and build on previous skills, while completing my Bachelor's degree.
